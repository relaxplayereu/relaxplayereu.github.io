SYNTHLUCIDA APPS

A collection of free, independent web apps by SYNTHLUCIDA. Every tool below runs standalone right in your browser and can be installed on your phone, tablet or computer like a native app — no App Store, no Google Play, no account required.

How to install any app from your browser:

  • Android (Chrome): open the app's page → tap the ⋮ menu in the top right → "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  • iPhone / iPad (Safari): open the app's page → tap the Share icon ⬆️ → "Add to Home Screen".
  • Desktop (Chrome / Edge): open the app's page → click the install icon ⊕ in the address bar → "Install".
