Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about streaming, offline listening, breathing tools and more.

Launch RelaxPlayer ← Back to RelaxPlayer
Is RelaxPlayer really free? +
Yes. RelaxPlayer streams in your browser at no cost, with no account, subscription or download required. Just open the page and press play.
What kind of music and sounds does RelaxPlayer play? +
A continuous rotation of ambient soundscapes, ASMR nature recordings (rain, waves, campfire, birdsong, thunderstorms), ambient piano pieces and slow chill/synth tracks, designed for focus, relaxation and sleep.
Do I need to create an account or install an app? +
No. RelaxPlayer runs directly in your browser on desktop and mobile. You can optionally add it to your home screen for an app-like experience, but no installation is required.
Can I use RelaxPlayer to help me sleep or study? +
Yes. Many listeners keep RelaxPlayer running in the background while studying, working or falling asleep. You can pick a calmer soundscape like rain or piano for sleep, or a steadier ambient track for focus.
Does RelaxPlayer work on mobile phones? +
Yes, the player is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktop computers, with a screen-on option so playback controls stay visible during long sessions.
Can I upload my own tracks and build custom playlists? +
Yes. The My Files section lets you upload your own audio and combine it with SYNTHLUCIDA tracks into playlists that stay saved in your browser.
Can I listen to RelaxPlayer offline? +
Yes. The Download Offline button saves the current playlist to your device, so you can keep listening without an internet connection.
What breathing techniques does RelaxPlayer offer? +
RelaxPlayer includes several guided techniques, such as 4-7-8, box, coherent, calming and sigh breathing, each shown as a pulsing visual guide you can follow.
What is the Card of the Day feature? +
It is a small daily tarot-style card, linked from the player, for a short moment of reflection alongside the music. No account or sign-up is required.
Can I chat with other listeners? +
Yes. A light community chat lets you share a short message and see reactions from other people listening at the same time.
