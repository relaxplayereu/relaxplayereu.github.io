TOOLKIT

More than a player

Alongside the 24/7 stream, RelaxPlayer bundles a few small tools for making the sound your own.

Launch RelaxPlayer ← Back to RelaxPlayer
📂

Your own playlists

Upload your own audio files and mix them with RelaxPlayer tracks into personal playlists you can come back to any time.

⬇️

Offline download

Download the current playlist to your device and keep listening even without an internet connection.

🃏

Card of the day

Draw a small daily tarot-style card for a short, reflective pause alongside the music — no account needed.

🫁

Guided breathing

Choose from several guided breathing techniques, including 4-7-8, box and coherent breathing, with a pulsing visual guide to follow.

🔔

Gentle reminders

Turn on local reminders that nudge you to pause and breathe every 15 minutes to 4 hours — nothing is ever sent to a server.

📈

Levels & progress

Build a simple listening streak that grows into levels and experience points, tracked entirely in your own browser.

💬

Community chat

Drop a few words into a light community chat and see reactions from other people listening right now.

🎮

SYNTHLUCIDA BLOCKS that unlocks music

Clear lines in a full SYNTHLUCIDA BLOCKS game to permanently unlock ASMR, piano and chill tracks — every 20 lines earns a bonus round and a bomb charge that instantly clears rows. Progress and visual theme are saved in your browser.

🌌

Interactive ambient night sky

Tap the night sky to play a generative synth, layer binaural waves and nature sounds, or build your own loops in a step sequencer. Set a sleep timer, record your session as a .wav file, and watch the sky shift with the real time of day and the actual moon phase.

🎨

Drawing canvas that comes alive

Paint with different brush feels, fill in coloring pages like mandalas and butterflies, or add stylised text and emoji. A soft ambient tone follows your brush as you draw, and marking a part of your picture lets you animate it — a wing that flaps, a star that twinkles — then replay or export it as video or GIF.
