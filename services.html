A personal, hands-on approach

Music and synthesizers are a genuine passion of mine. As an independent hobby producer — the same person behind the ambient and chillout tracks streaming on RelaxPlayer — I spend my free time exploring deep atmospheres and electronic soundscapes.

I don't run a large commercial studio. Instead, I offer a personal, authentic approach to every project. If you're a fellow creator looking for an atmospheric electronic touch, here's how we could work together.

What I offer

Four ways to bring a bit of atmospheric sound into your project.

01

Indie Music Production

Need original music for your project? I focus on atmospheric electronic, chillout and progressive styles, bringing genuine emotional depth to every track.

  • Perfect for: indie video creators, independent games, small podcasts or relaxation apps.
  • What you get: a custom-tailored, fully arranged electronic track built around the mood you're after.
02

Synth & Sound Design

I love digging deep into synthesizers to find unique, otherworldly tones. If a project feels a little empty, I can help fill the space.

  • What I offer: custom ambient drones, layered synth patches, atmospheric background textures and subtle sound effects.
03

Co-Production & Collabs

Have a great idea or a work-in-progress track but feel stuck? I'm always open to collaborating with other hobbyists and creators.

  • How it works: send over your stems or ideas — I can add missing synth layers, adjust the arrangement, or simply lend a fresh set of ears to help finish the track together.
04

Intro & Outro Audio

First impressions matter. Skip the generic stock music and get something that actually matches your vibe.

  • Perfect for: YouTubers, streamers and vloggers who want a short, memorable and original electronic audio identity.
